## SenseNova Loop 9B: Hands-On Test of a Looped Multimodal Model — fahdmirza

- https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_J4WyIZR8QA
- Fahd Mirza locally installs and tests SenseNova-Loop-9B-Preview, a nine-billion-parameter multimodal model that takes in text and images.
- Its looped architecture is built for long-context and agentic workflows, covering document understanding, coding, and external tool use.
- That a model this capable is runnable on local hardware matters: it keeps your data under your control and turns agentic workflows from a hosted service into something you can inspect and run yourself.

## Salesforce Just Helped Microsoft. You're Picking The Winner Of A Trillion-Dollar Fight. — NateBJones

- https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KDGxQtfjBj0
- Nate B. Jones examines why Salesforce helping Microsoft's agents, and Oracle bringing its applications into ChatGPT, are moves in a fight over who coordinates work across the software a business already owns.
- The prize is the agentic control plane: the layer that defines which agents can touch which data and what they are allowed to do.
- For anyone choosing AI tools today, interoperability is the thing to watch, because once a control plane is entrenched, switching ecosystems gets very hard.

## The "Ridiculous" Idea You're Afraid to Write Down — aishwaryasrinivasan

- https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UflYQQkt5uA
- Aishwarya Srinivasan makes the case for writing down the ambitious ideas you are tempted to dismiss as ridiculous before they ever leave your head.
- In a field moving as fast as AI, today's ridiculous idea is often tomorrow's product, and the builders who win are frequently just the ones who take an odd idea seriously a little longer.
- The practical takeaway is simple: capture the idea first and judge it later, because an idea thrown away in your head can never be developed.
