## OpenAI — V7 cuts costs 78% while boosting accuracy with GPT‑5.6 Luna
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=20IRGgQwPkc

V7 Go organizes company files into a Context Graph that agents can query to screen investment deals and support insurance underwriting. On V7's hardest graph-query tests across thousands of documents, GPT-6 Astra reached 89% accuracy — and GPT-5.6 Luna cut document-processing costs by 78% versus GPT-5.4 mini. ## The Next New Thing — AI Agents Finally Share One Memory (Claude Mem Explained)
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oGSShssjn9c

Alex Newman demonstrates Claude Mem, an open-source tool that connects AI agents through shared memory — so Claude, ChatGPT, and GrokBot could share memories and pick up each other's work without you repeating instructions. Agent memory is the missing layer of the agentic era, and this one is open source.

## Greg Isenberg — The 9 Stages of AI Agent Adoption, Explained
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nRl-7Yhmbog

Greg breaks down the war on AI agents: Amazon blocking Meta's Muse, Delta treating outside booking agents as unauthorized automated activity, Yelp charging agents for access. His framing — an agent as "a customer with a credit card who reads your site instead of looking at it" — plus a ladder of trust from research to prepare to pay, is the clearest map yet of how agent adoption will actually play out for businesses.

## LangChain — How Deep Agents Manages Context: Offloading and Summarization
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ud5RsBegyfw

The longer an agent runs, the worse its reasoning gets — "context rot." This video walks through two techniques the Deep Agents harness ships out of the box: offloading, which keeps large tool results out of context while saving them to a virtual file system, and summarization. The unsexy plumbing that decides whether your agent works on step fifty or falls apart on step five.
