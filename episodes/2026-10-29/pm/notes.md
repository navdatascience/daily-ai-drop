## Stop wiring infrastructure, start building agents. — Microsoft Developer

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AMxLLFeUyRM

Microsoft's Copilot harness in Foundry lets you build a prompt agent as a model, instructions, and tools, defined once and versioned — no container, no server, no pipeline. The pitch is that most agent projects rebuild the same scaffolding for no reason. It matters because starting simple and only paying the infrastructure cost when the agent earns it is the fastest path from demo to production.

## The Hardest Part of Testing Agents: Write Access — LangChain

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oY1htTx_h9U

LangChain PM Ben Tannyhill explains why read-only agents like Chat LangChain are easy to branch and test, while write-access agents that touch production data are still an unsolved evaluation problem for most teams. It matters because a wrong read-only agent breaks nothing, while a wrong write-access agent can change a database, close a ticket, or move money — testing that safely is the gap between a demo and an agent you trust near production.

## Two AI bots for spam calls and data privacy — Matthew Berman

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IrgQdsxzRa4

Berman looks at two AI bots aimed at spam calls and data privacy — AI showing up in the least glamorous, most personal places. It matters because if agents are going to earn trust, this is where they earn it: saving you from interruptions and keeping your information closer to home.

## SaaS is dead? — Greg Isenberg

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AqqjH_uv-_w

Isenberg argues that software as a service is dead, or at least breaking: for 15 years SaaS meant paying engineers to build a tool and charging a subscription, and AI that builds and customizes software on demand shifts value from the tool to the outcome. It matters even if you disagree, because every subscription product now has to answer what it does that an agent cannot just do for you.
