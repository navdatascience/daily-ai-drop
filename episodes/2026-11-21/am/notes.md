A quiet overnight with just two new videos, but they share a theme: how do you move fast and stay in control at the same time? Nate B. Jones looks at Microsoft's email outage and the recovery that made it worse, and Harrison Chase talks with LangChain about where agents are headed in 2027.

## When the fix makes the outage worse — Nate B. Jones

- **Video:** https://www.youtube.com/shorts/lcl9nu98AYI
- **Channel:** Nate B. Jones

Microsoft needed nearly a full day to restore email, and part of the delay came from the recovery itself: traffic rerouted to healthy servers took those servers down too. Nate connects the outage to a bigger shift — engineers are now defending against attackers who move at AI speed. The takeaway for anyone running production systems: your rollback plan needs a rollback plan, because speed without a safety margin turns a bad hour into a lost day.

## The Future of Agents with Harrison Chase — LangChain

- **Video:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GojQtxCT3dg
- **Channel:** LangChain

LangChain founder Harrison Chase on the next chapter of agent development. Organizations have moved past experimentation and are now trying to run agents in production, measure their impact, and judge whether they are worth the cost. The conversation covers context engineering as the central challenge of serious agent work, and the rise of proactive, always-on agents that act without being asked — which raises the question of how an agent knows when not to act, and who is accountable while you are not watching.
