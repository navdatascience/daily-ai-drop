## Fahd Mirza — Inworld Voice Isolator: Clean Noisy Voice Recordings Locally

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yjW6pnaOhT4

Inworld Voice Isolator strips background noise out of voice recordings, and it runs entirely on your own machine. That matters because nothing is uploaded to a cloud service, so sensitive recordings stay private, and because it turns a noisy take into a usable one instead of forcing a re-record. For podcasters, video creators, or anyone recording calls and interviews in an imperfect room, this is a quick cleanup step worth trying.

## LangChain — What Are Subagents? Explained in 4 Minutes

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AekKS44xBhI

Jess Weng explains subagents in Deep Agents, LangChain's open-source agent harness. A subagent takes one task from the main agent and does the messy work in its own fresh context window, sending back only the result — so the main agent's context stays focused on the big picture. Subagents can also run in parallel or in the background. If you are building or heavily using agents, this is the mental model that explains why multi-agent systems work instead of collapsing under their own context.
