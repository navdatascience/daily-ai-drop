## Fahd Mirza — GLM Legal Tested: Advancing Agentic Legal Intelligence

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=le_fe7zWsnY

Fahd tests GLM Legal, a model built specifically for legal work, on real-world legal workflows. Legal work demands precision, where a single word can change the meaning of a clause, so it is one of the toughest tests for any AI model. The video asks whether a specialized legal model can be trusted where generic chatbots fall short — a question that matters as law firms come under real pressure to adopt AI without risking sloppy output.

## Mehul Mohan — We need to talk about Theo

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Td7fDKcOyM8

Mehul digs into the T3 Code controversy: the coding tool ships with telemetry turned on by default and uses a stable hashed user ID that can identify you across sessions. He walks through how a login identifies you, why one stable ID per person raises eyebrows, and how the tool reads your Claude and Codex IDs. It matters because telemetry defaults are easy to miss and hard to undo — and transparency around data collection is becoming a deciding factor in which developer tools people trust.

## TheNextNewThingAI — Claude's insanely easy way to make apps

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZdmM3v6Yfz8

Claude's new App Maker can turn artifacts into desktop apps you can install on your Mac or Windows PC. ## Nate B. Jones — Why Gemini 4 feels better than ChatGPT 6.2

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IbBWjp-kLis

Nate compares ChatGPT 6.2 Astra and Gemini 4 Neon, arguing they represent a major shift in how users perceive AI quality. The argument is not about benchmark scores but about how a model feels to use day to day — tone, speed, and how often the answer lands the way you wanted. The two models split opinion, and that split says a lot about where the competition is heading: the model wars are now fought on experience, not just capability.
