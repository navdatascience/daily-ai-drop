## Why did OpenAI buy a drug development startup? — Aish Reganti

https://www.youtube.com/shorts/L2yYCoIYNdk

Aish Reganti explains OpenAI's acquisition of Orchestra Bio, a startup building AI agents that coordinate drug development. When a manufacturing batch fails, its agents find the affected trials, teams, and timelines and lay out the options, while people make the final call. It matters because getting one drug approved can take 10 to 15 years and cost more than $2.6 billion, much of it spent on trials, manufacturing, and approvals — exactly the kind of messy, high-stakes coordination where agents can help without replacing human judgment.
