## Mehul Mohan — BEST Way To Work With AI Agents (My NEW Setup)

## Aish Reganti — Is Jev dead? Why a16z still put $870M into it

https://www.youtube.com/shorts/HAjMrQAUxUA

Twenty copies of Jev showed up within a month of its launch, from Amazon, OpenAI, Cloudflare, Perplexity, and Microsoft — yet a16z still led an $870 million round. The bet is Jevons paradox: when decisions get dramatically cheaper, the market grows faster than the copycats can eat it. Being copied quickly may mean a startup found something real, not that it lost.

## Aishwarya Srinivasan — How to Retain Control & Autonomy in the Age of AI Agents | Sean White

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n_bxXEqkcSo

Aishwarya sits down with Sean White, CEO of Inflection AI, to discuss why emotional intelligence (EQ) and relational intelligence are becoming essential to the next generation of AI development. The conversation moves beyond IQ-focused models toward human-centric experiences built around trust and meaningful relationships. That matters because agents acting on our behalf need to be trusted like good assistants — and trust is built over time, not benchmarked.

## Fahd Mirza — Sage‑A1.5: Agentic Orchestration, Verification, and Open Scientific Discovery

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QhMWVQ6ZXbc

Fahd locally installs and tests Sage‑A1.5, a model aimed at open scientific discovery, focusing on agentic orchestration and verification. A science agent that checks its own work and orchestrates multi-step reasoning is far more useful than one that merely generates hypotheses. Running it locally also matters: open science tools only change science if researchers can run, inspect, and build on them directly.

## LangChain — The 50/50 problem with voice AI nobody is talking about #Shorts

https://www.youtube.com/shorts/f03D4dkwAdo

Zack Reneau-Wedeen, Head of Product at Sierra, breaks down a fundamental imbalance in voice AI: roughly 50 lines of Python decide when a voice agent speaks, while a trillion parameters decide what it says. Sierra's unlock was treating turn-taking as seriously as content generation — parallelizing thinking, listening, and talking the way humans naturally do. If you are building anything with voice, those 50 lines of turn-taking code may matter more than the trillion parameters behind the words.
