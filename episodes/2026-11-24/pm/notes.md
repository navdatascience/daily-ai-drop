## ERNIE 5.5 Flash: Driven by Baidu Dream — Fahd Mirza

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n_8q9p4dF2g

Fahd tests Baidu's new ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview hands-on. Baidu is pushing hard on speed and efficiency, and this is a useful check on how open competition in frontier models is shaping up outside the US labs.

## Ruflo — Turn Claude Code Into a Whole AI Dev Team — TheNextNewThingAI

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QhoQ9S9cUKI

Ruflo, by ruvnet, is an agent harness that runs multiple LLMs together as a coordinated team of developers: one plans, others build, another reviews, with a manager layer keeping them in sync. It matters because it moves coding agents from a single assistant to a team that can parallelize real work.

## I let AI agents run my entire podcast — TheNextNewThingAI

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qU0rrMr_ag8

Andrew Warner talks with Kendall Breitman about Riverside's new AI agents, which can book guests, record interviews, edit video, create clips, and publish. A glimpse of what a fully automated creator workflow looks like — and a test of whether agents can run a production end to end.

## 5 ways to get the most out of Gemini 4 Neon — Greg Isenberg

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dk8vltIu_6k

Greg breaks down Gemini 4 Neon, Google's new fast frontier model, after testing it inside Antigravity. He shows real builds from the community, including motion design work, and five practical ways to squeeze more out of the model.

## Robots vs Self Driving — Greg Isenberg

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e_QqhvBXihI

Greg sits down again with Adrien Gaidon, co-founder and chief strategy officer of Walden Robotics, to map out where the openings sit in robotics and physical AI. They dig into how teams teach robots with data, why deployment data carries the most value, and why Walden picked wheels and two-finger grippers for factory work. A follow-up to this morning's robotics founder interview, this time on where robots win first versus self-driving.

## The AI bubble is bigger than you think, Jensen is back! — Nate B. Jones (#shorts)

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=S1COTnDviGc

Nate argues the AI trade has grown so large it now requires consistent support, drawing parallels to the 2008 financial crisis. A short, sharp warning about systemic risk in the AI boom.

## Don't Call It a Decision Model: Allie Laabs on Jev and System One — LangChain

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T5ULj8VEJZo

Allie Laabs, founding developer advocate at TypeSafe AI, explains why the company calls its model Jev a System One model, after Daniel Kahneman's term for the quick, intuitive judgments a knowledgeable person makes in seconds. With a customer support routing example, she shows how putting a whole decision into one Jev question avoids prompt gymnastics.

## Don't Lock Your Agent Harness Into a Single Model Provider — LangChain

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1Gg4ejXFAYw

Harrison Chase, LangChain's co-founder and CEO, argues an agent harness should be model neutral, so you can switch to the best new model as soon as it comes out and avoid getting locked into a single provider that can raise rates. He points to LangGraph and Deep Agents, both open source, as ways to keep control of what the harness does.

## Inside LangChain's GTM agent: one agent, 47 use cases — LangChain

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ID6SE3eY3PY

Danny Lambert, who leads GTM engineering at LangChain, walks through the GTM agent his team built on Deep Agents. It started by drafting personalized emails for inbound leads and is now one central agent with 47 graphs that half the company uses every week. A real production agent story, not a demo, with routing-by-role lessons for anyone building agents at work.

## Building Agent Harnesses with Jev: Where Decision Models Fit — LangChain

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qquIt--ANqI

Sydney Runkle walks through where decision models like TypeSafe AI's Jev fit in an agent harness — the layer around the core agent loop where much of the logic runs on extra LLM calls that slow the application down. In one LangChain A/B test, routed threads took much longer than unrouted ones because of the routing step, which is exactly the problem Jev is built to solve.

## Jensen Huang back as NVIDIA's CEO — Aishwarya Srinivasan

Aishwarya looks at the news around Jensen Huang and NVIDIA's leadership. NVIDIA sits at the center of the whole AI buildout, so any shift in how Huang leads is news for everyone in this space.

## Experiment with SVG & 3D Design with the Codex Plugin — OpenAI

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1u0hTd86_HU

Craig Dennis had never drawn an SVG by hand or used Blender. With the SVG & 3D Design plugin for Codex, he turns a reference image into an editable graphic or 3D model, then asks for changes in plain language — including a character whose eyes follow your cursor — while Codex uses Blender to check the model. It lowers the bar for design work considerably.

## How to Search for Exoplanets — OpenAI

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p8lvQQebvpg

Two amateur astronomers, Kevin Bond and Ethan Byrd, independently found signals that could point to planets around distant stars. Kevin used Codex and Ethan used Astra to search public telescope data for tiny, recurring dips in starlight that an orbiting planet can cause. A reminder that frontier models are also science tools: anyone with curiosity and public data can join the hunt.
