## Alpie Seed 9B Tested: Not Every 9B Model Deserves the Hype — Fahd Mirza

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bhl3P-C54VU

Fahd installs and tests Alpie Seed 9B, a vision model for reasoning from research group 169Pi, openly available on Hugging Face. At nine billion parameters it sits in the middleweight class: small enough to run on your own hardware, big enough that people expect real capability from it. The video is a hands-on reality check on that promise — the only way to learn whether a model is useful or just well marketed.

Why it matters: nine-billion-parameter models keep showing up because they promise the best of both worlds, capable reasoning and local control, and that makes them the perfect size for hype. Open availability means anyone can download and test these models, so the question is no longer whether open models exist but which ones are actually good. Fahd's test is the filter: a model earns its hype by surviving a real install and real tasks, not a launch announcement.
