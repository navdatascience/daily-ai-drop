## Gemini 4 Neon: Google's Middle Model Tested — Fahd Mirza

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sZU2I8_uoeg

Fahd puts Google's new middle-tier Gemini 4 model, Neon, through a demanding mega test prompt to see how it handles complexity. The middle model is the one most people will actually use, so how it performs decides the real trade-off between capability and cost. If Neon holds up under a serious test, it becomes the practical default for a lot of builds — and it signals that Google's full model ladder is strong enough that the middle rung is no longer a compromise.

## Meet Brian and Beast, the dots at work at Holiday Extras — OpenAI

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ikNf7eW8CHk

OpenAI shows its Dots personal agents inside a real company, Holiday Extras. David Lee, Director of Innovation and Growth, calls his dot Brian on his morning commute to talk through the meetings ahead, and Brian has a report ready by the time he reaches his desk. His colleague Liam Thompson, VP of AI Innovation, calls his dot Beast — the day after Holiday Extras launched a new app feature, Beast checked how it was performing and brought Liam the findings without a manual report run. This is agents moving out of demos and into real offices, with names, personalities, and actual jobs.
