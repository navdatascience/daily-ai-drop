## Podium's Playbook for Scaling AI Agents Org-Wide — LangChain

Walker Ward of Podium explains why making LangSmith the default agent platform across the whole company was a bigger unlock than any single feature. ## Your Password Can Be Hacked: Here's the Fix — pvergadia
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_8csmLr4Wws

Priyanka walks through how passwords authenticate users, why multi-factor authentication can still fall to phishing and man-in-the-middle attacks, and how attackers steal and replay session cookies. The fix: device-bound cryptographic authentication — private keys tied to your physical device, bound to a specific session — so a stolen cookie is useless. A jargon-free explainer of the post-password future.

## Thomson Reuters on Building Trust in Legal AI — OpenAI
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ExVDpMeaWp0

Camillo Speroni of Thomson Reuters sets the bar for legal AI: output lawyers can verify, cite, and put their own name on. He discusses Thomson Reuters' work with OpenAI to bring HighQ and CoCounsel Legal into ChatGPT Enterprise, connecting the tools lawyers already rely on. Legal AI is moving from experiment to daily workflow — and trust is the whole ballgame.

## Meta Muse Will Create 1,000 Millionaires — Greg Isenberg
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tpZOU1ugODQ

Greg argues you can use Meta Muse to mine Facebook groups, Marketplace, and Instagram to match buyers with sellers — arbitrage as a business. The idea came after the night-nurse agency he hired admitted it finds candidates in Facebook groups. Meta owns the social graph and the marketplaces; an agent living on top of that graph will mint brokers and micro-businesses at scale.

## Perplexity's pplx-decider-v1.2: Is It Worth Using? We Tested It Live — Fahd Mirza
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ph62tsCwExY

Fahd tests Perplexity's new decision model live — the follow-up to pplx-decider-v1.1-27b, built on the same Qwen3.8-27B backbone — with no editing, so you see the hits and the misses. Routing (which model handles which query) is quietly one of AI's most important problems, and this is the honest benchmark for whether the upgrade is worth it.

## AnyPS5 — This AI Rewrites a PS5 Game to Run on Your PC — TheNextNewThingAI
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BL3UQPoPG7Y

AnyPS5 is an open-source tool that uses AI to read a PlayStation 5 game's files and convert them into a version that runs natively on Windows or Linux. It isn't an emulator — it actually rewrites the game. A signpost for AI-powered reverse engineering, raising questions about game preservation and the legal gray zone of converting games you own.

## REA use cases you'll love — TheNextNewThingAI
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BctbCJP9lKU

Andrew Warner tests REA (Reverse Engineer Anything), showing how coding agents can inspect closed-source apps and learn how features work without the source code. Incredible for security research and learning — and unsettling for any business model that depends on keeping software's inner workings secret.
