## Introducing ChatGPT Images 2.6 — OpenAI

## Ripple-65B: Thin and Deep Dense Model, Tested Locally — Fahd Mirza

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gd5tnklXxLY

## I built my own PDF reader to replace Adobe Acrobat — Matthew Berman

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t8-RG3ju_Ao

Matthew builds his own PDF reader as an alternative to Acrobat, one of those tools everybody pays for and nobody loves. The bigger point: the software we treat as unchangeable is increasingly rebuildable with modern AI tooling. If you read research papers or reports all day, a reader tuned to your own habits is genuinely appealing.

## Did AI make my $100K CMU master's useless? — Aish Reganti

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dgAb-QWmVbw

Aish did her master's at Carnegie Mellon, the top-ranked graduate computer science program, and says she would still go — for a different reason than most people expect. Drawing on Randy Pausch's idea of "head fakes," she argues the degree was never really about the lectures, but about the people, the pressure, and the environment. A useful reframing if you're weighing a degree, a bootcamp, or learning on your own.

## 14 Day Roadmap to learn AI Agents — Aishwarya Srinivasan

A focused two-week, step-by-step plan for learning AI agents, from the basics to building something real. Agents are the skill everybody is hiring for and nobody quite knows how to teach yet — a structured fourteen-day plan beats another month of bookmarked tutorials you never open.
