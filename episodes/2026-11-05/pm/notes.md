# The Daily AI Drop — Evening Edition, November 5, 2026

A big afternoon: open-source Kimi K3.1 weights, Azure SQL beating Aurora on throughput, GPT-6 Cyber securing Firefox, five hundred hours of fine-tuning lessons, and cheap AI video with sound. Alex and Jordan run through nine new videos from the channels.

## Kimi K3.1 weights OUT, open source computer use, SOTA image model, science model, Pika H3: AI NEWS — Matthew Berman

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-M62vWrZh54

Matthew rounds up a wave of open-source releases, headlined by Kimi K3.1's weights going public, plus open-source computer use, a state-of-the-art image model, a science model, and Pika H3 for video. When frontier-class weights drop publicly, every developer can inspect, fine-tune, and build on them the same day. The gap between closed labs and the open community keeps shrinking in weeks, not years.

## 52% More Throughput. 1.5x Better Value. The Azure SQL DB Hyperscale Advantage | Data Exposed — Microsoft Developer

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FtpOReqx5ts

Principled Technologies benchmarked Azure SQL Database Hyperscale against Amazon Aurora PostgreSQL on an I/O-intensive workload and found 52% more throughput and 1.5x better value. AI workloads hammer the database layer, so throughput and price-performance at the data tier decide how much AI you can actually afford to run. Worth a look before choosing cloud infrastructure for an AI product.

## How Mozilla strengthens Firefox security with GPT-6 Cyber — OpenAI

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pXRZyOxbDL4

Mozilla used GPT-6 Cyber to uncover previously unknown high-severity vulnerabilities in Firefox and identify architectural improvements, then shipped fixes to millions of users. This is AI security work with a concrete payoff: real flaws in a browser used by hundreds of millions of people, found and fixed. Here the model is the guard, doing deep code review that would take a human team weeks.

## Stop wasting time: How to value your hours #time #efficiency #tips — Nate B. Jones

https://www.youtube.com/shorts/XuUEgWtLfMw

Nate asks a deceptively simple question: is saving two hours worth ten dollars to you, or three hours worth fifty? Most of us never put a dollar value on an hour of our own time, so we can't decide what to delegate to an agent. Pick a number and the delegation decision gets easy — and he shares an interview prompt to help you price your hour.

## I Spent 500+ Hours Fine-Tuning AI. Here's What I Learned — Aish Reganti

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m5FsWlrwFJM

After more than five hundred hours of fine-tuning, Aish asks whether fine-tuning is dead. OpenAI is winding down its self-service fine-tuning platform, yet Thinking Machines, Mistral, and Fireworks are investing heavily in it — so fine-tuning isn't dying, it's moving. Her takeaway: fine-tuning is becoming a specialist tool for making a model behave in a very specific way, not a default step. Her lessons will save you from learning them the slow way.

## Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash: Fast and Cheap Video Clips with Sound — Fahd Mirza

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jpl7l7GUb_4

Fahd tests Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash on fifteen-second video clips with sound and finds it fast and cheap. Video with sound has been the expensive, slow part of the pipeline; a model that does short clips quickly and cheaply changes who can experiment. Fifteen seconds is the perfect length for social posts, ads, and prototypes — and low cost means iterating ten times instead of once.

## A 4B AI model that creates 3D game assets — Matthew Berman

https://www.youtube.com/shorts/hu1YDJZYGTc

A four-billion-parameter model that generates 3D game assets — small enough to run on consumer hardware. Game development has always been bottlenecked by asset creation, so a compact model producing usable 3D objects on demand lets indie teams build worlds that used to need a studio.

## New AI creates YouTube videos, AI makes calls, & all the best releases — The Next New Thing AI

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-s4rbKbrO0w

Andrew Warner and Corey Ganim run through the latest releases: AI-generated documentaries, a personalized YouTube experience, silent speech with AirPods, agent-powered shopping, and tools that make coding agents smarter. The thread connecting them: AI is moving from chat windows into everyday experiences, and the interface is disappearing. The silent-speech detail is the one to watch — an assistant you can talk to without speaking out loud.

## AI identified Breakout from 17,253 numbers — Matthew Berman

https://www.youtube.com/shorts/F4i_-wbmhC8

An AI identified the game Breakout from a stream of 17,253 numbers — no images, no screenshots. A small party trick with a serious point: models are getting very good at recognizing structure in raw data, even when it doesn't look like anything to us. Pattern recognition is the quiet superpower behind all of this, from numbers on a screen to vulnerabilities in a browser.
