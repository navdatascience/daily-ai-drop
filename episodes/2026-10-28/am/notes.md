## The Context Window Problem Nobody Talks About — Aish Reganti

https://www.youtube.com/shorts/U1S4FvRFRiY

Aish breaks down why long AI conversations quietly lose track of earlier instructions and decisions, and how context compaction preserves what matters. Techniques like sliding windows help an assistant hold onto critical decisions over long sessions instead of only remembering the last few messages — a mental model worth having the next time you design a prompt or build an agent.

## Deepseek crushed everyone — Matthew Berman

## Legora legal AI series — OpenAI (four videos)

- Faster contract review. Broader legal analysis. | Legora's Alex Fortescue-Webb: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=z-WZMjNsg-k
- Building the future of legal work with AI | Legora CEO Max Junestrand: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UOfmD1fY-i8
- From legal bottleneck to business partner | Legora's Emma Kadri Bergström: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=M2Oa42hF67E

OpenAI's four-part Legora series shows a working legal platform using OpenAI models, ChatGPT, and Codex. General counsel Emma Kadri Bergström says NDA reviews once took around 20% of her team's capacity and now take under 1%, with lawyers staying responsible for decisions. Global head of legal engineering Alex Fortescue-Webb explains how teams can analyze thousands of contracts across multiple questions in minutes, expanding due diligence, while CEO Max Junestrand frames the ambition of making legal expertise available wherever it is needed. Together, the series is a concrete example of AI returning expert time for higher-value work rather than just demoing a feature.
