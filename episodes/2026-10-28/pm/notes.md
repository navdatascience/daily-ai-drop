## What is LangChain? — LangChain

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rfhhhmajCKc

On their own, large language models are isolated from your data and your tools. LangChain is the open-source framework that connects them to what they need to actually get things done, moving from hand-designed workflows to agents that loop through reasoning and tool calls. It is a clear starting point if you are new to building agents.

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EAEixlPC6L4

## Epic Games' RAD Debugger Fills AI's Biggest Gap in Game Dev — The Next New Thing

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mzLkzIF1Te8

The RAD Debugger is an open-source, graphical debugger from Epic Games, a native, user-mode, multi-process debugger for local-machine x64 debugging on Windows and Linux. It matters for AI because AI can code games but is still not great at visual debugging — closing that gap helps humans and agents find problems they can actually see.
