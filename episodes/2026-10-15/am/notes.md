## Videos covered

### Fahd Mirza — LFM2.5 24B-A2B: Did Liquid AI Manage to Beat Qwen? Tested Locally
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gml5Hlca-Qs

Fahd installs and tests Liquid AI's LFM2.5 24B-A2B entirely on local hardware — no cloud, no API keys. The A2B mixture-of-experts design activates roughly 2B parameters per token, which is what makes it fast enough for agentic work on consumer-grade machines. Why it matters: local agents only work at scale if they're cheap and private — no latency, no per-call bills, no data leaving the building. Whether or not it dethrones Qwen outright, having multiple credible open options in the fast-local-agent category is the real story.

### AI News & Strategy Daily | Nate B Jones — Is AI making us really productive?
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ShSVKyAjyyk

Nate argues AI can make distraction look extremely productive: by 5 PM you have prototypes, plans, and test versions of ideas you weren't even pursuing that morning — but little that got far enough to make a difference to anybody. Why it matters: when starting projects becomes nearly free, finishing becomes the scarce skill. Taste and follow-through, not output volume, are the premium career skills in the AI era.
