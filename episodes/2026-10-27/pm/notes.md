## How to Build Agents That Improve Over Time: Model, Harness, Context | Interrupt London

- Channel: LangChain
- Link: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xy_7SzwsIjM

## MCP 2.0 Is Here: What Changed in Model Context Protocol

- Channel: Aishwarya Srinivasan
- Link: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mawskzieSbg

A walkthrough of six major updates in Model Context Protocol 2.0: stateless requests, multi-roundtrip workflows with human approval, more reliable streaming and progress updates, intelligent caching, and enterprise authorization.
