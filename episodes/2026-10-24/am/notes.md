## Aish Reganti — Prompt, context, harness, loop, graph engineering explained

## Aishwarya Srinivasan — Why College is Dying and AI is Eating Software | Deedy Das

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pQ1AJEvV9z4

A conversation with venture capitalist Deedy Das on how investors now evaluate AI startups, why traditional software moats are breaking down, and how the next generation is bypassing college to build at the frontier. The credential matters less; the portfolio of things you have actually built matters more.
