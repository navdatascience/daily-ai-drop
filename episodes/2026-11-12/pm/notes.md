## Cheaper AI can lead to more spending

**Channel:** matthew_berman
**Video:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G11-Vmw7-PQ

Matthew Berman takes on the paradox that cheaper AI leads to higher spending. It is the Jevons paradox applied to tokens: when something gets cheaper, people do much more of it, so total spend climbs even as the unit price falls. If you are budgeting for AI next year, that dynamic matters more than the sticker price.

## VideoLingo — Dub Any Webinar Into Another Language With This Open-Source AI

**Channel:** TheNextNewThingAI
**Video:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hbzicDw4xZw

VideoLingo is a free, open-source tool that translates, subtitles, and dubs videos into other languages automatically — smart enough not to translate people's names. The business idea is simple: take a company's webinars and demos, translate them for a new market, and build a sales operation on top, taking a share of the revenue. For any company selling software internationally, dubbing is the cheapest new market entry there is.

## Turn Meeting Videos Into Shorts for Free With This AI Tool

**Channel:** TheNextNewThingAI
**Video:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NDo3b7R6gDM

A free, open-source tool turns long meeting recordings into vertical shorts, using a language model to find highlights and Whisper to transcribe, with automatic cropping, no watermarks, and no per-clip credits. It is an open-source alternative to Opus Clip and similar tools. The advice is to record meetings, let the tool find the great moments, and post a lot — a few clips will take off, and it is a good fit for people who write well on LinkedIn and X but have never made video.

## Build smarter search without AI: Fuzzy matching for applications | Data Exposed

**Channel:** MicrosoftDeveloper
**Video:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kaOI6ibSMwo

Not every search problem needs AI, embeddings, or vector databases. Most business applications just need to handle misspelled customer names, inconsistent product descriptions, duplicate records, and incomplete entries. The newest fuzzy string-matching capabilities in Azure SQL and SQL Server handle typo-tolerant search, duplicate detection, and data cleansing without introducing AI complexity. Sometimes the unglamorous tool is the right tool — and the cheapest one.
