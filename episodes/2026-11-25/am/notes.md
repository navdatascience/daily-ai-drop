# The Daily AI Drop — Morning Edition, November 25, 2026

Two videos dropped overnight: Microsoft's new on-device AI model gets a hands-on test, and a sticky note doodle becomes a short film.

## Microsoft Aion-1 2.3B: Hands-On Test of Windows' New On-Device AI — Fahd Mirza

- Watch: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q9olIRcN-Xg
- Published: November 25, 2026, 12:00 AM CST

Microsoft's Aion-1 is a 2.3-billion-parameter small language model built to run locally on Windows, and Fahd installs Aion Instruct and puts it through its paces. On-device AI matters because your prompts and documents never leave your machine, responses skip the round trip to a data center, and you stop paying per token for everyday tasks. It is an early checkpoint on Microsoft's vision of Windows as an AI platform — if small models get good enough for ordinary work, the economics of AI and who controls your data both shift.

## I turned a sticky note doodle into a short film — Mehul Mohan

- Watch: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZyxUwYiK6kU
- Published: November 25, 2026, 3:50 AM CST

Mehul starts with a photo of a doodle on a sticky note and uses ElevenCreative (from ElevenLabs) to turn it into two images, animate them into video, and generate the music plus rain and wave sounds, then cuts it all together in his editor. It is a single-project demo of the full AI creative chain — image, video, and audio generation working together — with almost no barrier to entry: a pen, a sticky note, and a tool that carries an idea from one medium to the next. The video also ties into the Year of More competition, which offers $200,000 in total prizes and is open to free and paid users with no purchase required.
