## Claude Code Can Now Grow Your Website Traffic — TheNextNewThingAI

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H3rmW6CrROU

MeasureScale is an MCP server that connects Claude, ChatGPT, Codex and GrokBot directly to Google Analytics, Search Console, Google Ads and Google Drive, so the agent can look at your real traffic data and act on it instead of you pasting spreadsheets into a chatbot. ## Microsoft, The FAA And Even The NSA Had Outages In One Week. What Is Happening? — Nate B. Jones

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fFeyE4nINqs

Nate zooms out from the Microsoft email outage to a whole week of failures at Microsoft, the FAA, banks and the NSA. His framing: AI cyber defense is now a race between attackers who can get deep access in as little as 15 minutes and defenders who need days or weeks to put systems back. It matters because repair speed has not kept up with attack speed, and AI is widening that gap on both sides — the clearest explanation yet of why these outages keep clustering.

## DaisyRose 27B Locally: Pushing the Boundaries of Original Qwen — Fahd Mirza

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yDQBz77Jzpc

RadixArk's first model is a multimodal reasoning model built on Qwen3.8-27B and trained with reinforcement learning only — no supervised fine-tuning stage. It matters because a 27-billion-parameter reasoning model you can run yourself was ambitious a year ago; now new teams are shipping them with unusual training recipes, and this is the road test.

## How to design an agent harness — Greg Isenberg

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LrJ4RYRQSpk

Jon Noronha, co-founder and chief product officer of Gamma, explains agent harnesses — the scaffolding that turns a raw language model into a product that does a job — and how Gamma rebuilt itself as a creative agent this year, with roughly 20 models and 200 calls behind one finished presentation. It matters for anyone building agents: in creative work, Jon argues taste matters more than evals, and that judgment is where the product wins or loses.
