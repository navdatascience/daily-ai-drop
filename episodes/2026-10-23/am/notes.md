## Fahd Mirza — [InclusionAI Releases 2 Models in a Day: Ling-3.0 Flash Sante Tested](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0OO9jogP4Y)

Fahd tests Ling-3.0-flash-Sante, a healthcare-specialized model built on Ling-3.0-flash — one of two models InclusionAI dropped in a single day. The headline isn't just a new checkpoint; it's the pace of the open-weight race. A healthcare-tuned, inspectable open model matters because medical domains are where generic models hallucinate the hardest. Worth benchmarking if you build in health tech.

## Nate B. Jones — [This is the best AI news #blackbox #AI](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Xo1oF84Dhq8)

A follow-up to yesterday's Goodfire story (see the October 22 evening edition): Nick Cammarata's essay "Neural Networks Are Already Clean" argues neurons that fire for both cats and cars are actually doing one coherent job — the neuron isn't polysemantic noise, it's a V-shape detector. Nate doubles down despite loud pushback from Neel Nanda and Leo Gao and the absence of a technical paper so far. If interpretability is advancing faster than expected, deception detection and AI safety get real tools sooner than the pessimists predicted.

## The Cloud Girl (Priyanka Vergadia) — [Reverse Engineering Claude Code Harness: Best Practices Harness Engineering](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tuk6gh0Aqfo)

Priyanka traces a single coding task through the entire Claude Code system to show what happens when you hit enter: the model doesn't execute commands directly — a harness manages the agent loop, tools, memory, permissions, and hooks. Her point: harness engineering matters as much as model choice, and it's where production agents live or die. In a week of agent deep-dives (Claude Duet, memory layers), this is the required-viewing builder episode.
