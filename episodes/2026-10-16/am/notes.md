## Fahd Mirza — "Ternary Bonsai 2 9B in GGUF: A 2.4 GB Model Locally"

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y7mGNH4-zJI

Fahd installs and tests Bonsai 2 Ternary 9B, a nine-billion-parameter vision-language model compressed into a GGUF file of just 2.4 GB. Ternary weights (each weight reduced to three possible values) make a serious model runnable on ordinary local hardware — no data center, no big GPU. This matters because a vision-language model this small can run entirely offline, reading screenshots and documents with no data leaving the machine and no cloud bill — the strongest signal yet that ternary quantization is practical, not just a research curiosity.

## pvergadia — "Your Password & MFA Can Still Be Hacked: Here's the Fix"

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nmBtnePhMHE

Passwords were supposed to be the wall, then MFA the second wall — but modern phishing steals the whole authenticated session, so even MFA-enabled accounts can be hijacked after login. The video explains how session hijacking works and argues the real fix is device-bound cryptography (passkeys, hardware keys): credentials cryptographically tied to your device, so there's nothing typed or transmitted for a phishing site to steal. The takeaway: MFA beats passwords, but the future is getting rid of shared secrets entirely.
