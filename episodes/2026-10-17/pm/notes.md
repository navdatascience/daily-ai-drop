## The Next New Thing AI — Amazon and others want to block your agents

## Fahd Mirza — VoiceStudio: Local Voice AI That Doesn't Beat ElevenLabs

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yNy5-akjFMg

Fahd installs and tests VoiceStudio, an open-source voice AI suite that runs entirely on your own machine: voice cloning, voice design, video dubbing, dictation, and transcription. It doesn't quite beat ElevenLabs — the gold standard — but it gets remarkably close with no API bills and no voice data leaving your hardware. A follow-up to this morning's Fish Audio Drama 3 local test, and more evidence that pro-grade audio capability is moving from the cloud to the laptop.

## Fahd Mirza — DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017 Hands-On: Bug Fixing, Game Building and Image Reasoning
