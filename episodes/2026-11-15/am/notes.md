## Why "Act Like an Expert" Can Make Your AI Prompts Worse — Aish Reganti

https://www.youtube.com/shorts/dT-AGx5tR04

Wharton researchers tested prompts like "You are a world-class expert" across six AI models on hard questions in physics, chemistry, engineering, and law. Expert personas didn't reliably improve accuracy — and on one benchmark, nine comparisons came out significantly worse. Low-knowledge personas like "toddler" lowered accuracy in four of six models. The takeaway: a persona is a shortcut that can cost you accuracy; plain, specific prompts with real context and clear constraints work better than roleplay.

## MiniMax H3 Image in ComfyUI Tested Locally — Fahd Mirza

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8Z3pedHd6Vo

Fahd installs and tests the new MiniMax-H3-Image model locally inside ComfyUI, running it entirely on his own hardware. Another sign of the shrinking gap between open models you can run at home and the big labs' offerings.
