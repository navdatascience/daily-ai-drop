### Fahd Mirza — "LongCat 2.5 from Meituan: What We Saw in Two Tests"
- Link: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9ZfyTFgGjTA
- Published: November 15, 2026, 12:00 PM CT
- LongCat 2.5 is Meituan's native multimodal mixture-of-experts model, available on Hugging Face — built from the ground up to handle text and images together, activating only a subset of its parameters per task.
- Fahd tested it locally on his own hardware, continuing his weekend streak of hands-on open-model testing after MiniMax H3 Image this morning and Alpie Seed 9B last night. A local test is the reality check that matters: it shows what a model is actually like to use, not what its launch announcement claims.
- It matters because the race is no longer just about the smartest text model — it's about one model that sees, reads, and reasons together. A native multimodal MoE is one of the most promising architectures for that, and having it openly available lets independent developers poke at it, fine-tune it, and figure out what it's actually good for.
