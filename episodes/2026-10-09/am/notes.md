## PG-Jev: Query Your Database in Plain English with Decision Model — Fahd Mirza (fahdmirza)

- **Video:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XiGBCk5MnnY
- **Published:** 2026-10-09 01:00 America/Chicago

Fahd installs and tests PG-Jev, a PostgreSQL extension powered by TypeSafe's Jev decision model, which turns plain-English questions into database queries. The important detail is that it runs locally, next to your database, so sensitive data never has to leave your own infrastructure. The real test is not basic SQL generation — it is whether the model stays accurate when your schema is messy, with years-old abbreviations and table names only one person understands.

## Why AI Agents Work So Well in Finance & Coding — pvergadia

- **Video:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H0RXQWcDdQs
- **Published:** 2026-10-09 06:30 America/Chicago

Priya's answer to why agents thrive in coding and finance comes down to mathematical verification. In coding, an agent can write code, compile it, and run the tests to find out immediately whether it worked. In finance, outcomes can likewise be checked mathematically. That automatic grading loop is what makes reliable agents possible — and it raises the useful question of which other industries can verify answers just as cheaply.

## How Sophos cuts threat response time by 96% with OpenAI Daybreak — OpenAI

- **Video:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MUHjyljBPHI
- **Published:** 2026-10-09 03:37 America/Chicago

Sophos CTO John Peterson explains how agents built through OpenAI Daybreak combine AI with Sophos's cybersecurity expertise to cut average threat response time from 38 minutes to 89 seconds for cases using those agents — a 96% reduction. The agents help the MDR team investigate threats faster and protect customers at scale, while human judgment stays at the center of every decision. Security telemetry acts as the verifier: the agent's work can be checked against real data.

## Tesla Powerwall and Car Backup During an Outage — Matthew Berman (matthew_berman)

- **Video:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=o_gf-LEaOWw
- **Published:** 2026-10-08 23:10 America/Chicago

Matthew documents what actually happened when the power went out and his Tesla Powerwall, supplemented by his Tesla vehicle as a backup source, had to carry the house. It is a practical look at the questions that matter — how much load the setup can carry, what to prioritize, and how long the backup lasts. The underlying theme is home energy as a scheduling and optimization problem: solar, storage, and an EV together turn an outage from a crisis into an inconvenience.
