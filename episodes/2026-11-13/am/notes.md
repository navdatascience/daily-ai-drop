Three new videos overnight, all circling the same question from different directions: who gets to decide? Matthew Berman asks who controls access to AI itself, Fahd Mirza shows how to build your own routing layer with vLLM's Semantic Router, and Priyanka Vergadia explains why great employees don't automatically become great managers.

## Who controls access to AI? — Matthew Berman

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yVaVR2Ywfik

A small handful of companies and governments currently decide who gets to use the most capable models, what those models may do, and what they cost. Matthew maps the levers — closed model weights, API pricing, export rules — and why access terms shape what every builder can do. If you build with AI, the terms of access decide what you can ship and whether your product survives the next pricing change.

## vLLM Semantic Router: Programmable AI Routing Layer for Models — Fahd Mirza

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WSWPn50xXus

A hands-on local tutorial on vLLM's Semantic Router, a programmable layer that decides which model should answer each request. Simple questions go to a small, fast, cheap model; hard reasoning goes to the heavyweight; domain-specific requests go to a specialist. For anyone running models in production, routing is the practical way to keep AI spending under control without giving up capability.

## Being a Great Employee Doesn't Mean You'll Be a Great Manager! — Priyanka Vergadia

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iB4E2TvEuhE

The skills that make you excellent at doing the work are not the same skills that make you excellent at leading the people who do it. Priyanka walks through the leadership qualities companies actually look for — setting direction, giving actionable feedback, deciding with incomplete information — and why they matter more than ever as AI reshapes teams faster than org charts can keep up.
