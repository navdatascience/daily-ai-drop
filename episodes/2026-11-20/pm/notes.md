## Anthropic Ran 1,000 AI Agents For 8 Days. Yours Still Needs The Same Thing Explained Again. — Nate B. Jones

## Clay's Head of AI on Reading Traces With Hundreds of Tool Calls — LangChain

- https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tPszikxVpgE
- Jeff Barg, Head of AI at Clay, talks with Harrison Chase about debugging agent traces that can run to hundreds of tool calls. Pasting a raw trace into a coding tool and asking what went wrong works surprisingly badly; asking the model to summarize each command first goes much better.

- https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UaL_BNP4bTE
