## Meet the Data Agent in ChatGPT — OpenAI

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KrlfehIapEA

OpenAI introduces the Data plugin in ChatGPT: ask questions about your data in plain language and get visualizations, dashboards, and reports back. The plugin works with your files and connected tools and includes skills for checking data quality, defining KPIs, and business analysis.

## Create and Share a Report in ChatGPT Space — OpenAI

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3_fayTbZrW8

This walkthrough turns a fictional Acme sales analysis into a Page in Space, with highlights, quarterly comparisons, and charts in one place. You choose who can access the report, copy a link for your meeting, edit a heading directly, ask ChatGPT questions about the report, and leave comments beside the findings. It matters because it turns a one-off AI answer into a living document your team can discuss and work on together.

## Create and Share a Dashboard in ChatGPT — OpenAI

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OSHSopaYjTw

Building on the same Acme sales example, this video shows how to turn analysis into a dashboard your team can use day to day, with quarterly metrics and customer follow-ups in one view. You can inspect the data behind a chart, edit its appearance, ask questions about the numbers, and refine the dashboard with annotations. ## Ask Questions About Your Data in ChatGPT — OpenAI

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JEgTn37Sk50

A practical guide to preparing for a sales meeting by asking ChatGPT what changed in your data and which customers need attention. Using sample Acme data, the walkthrough compares quarterly sales, visualizes the segments behind the change, and explores customer orders alongside account notes. ## Connect Your Team's Data to ChatGPT — OpenAI

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oJIuz3AFfEQ

A walkthrough for workspace admins on connecting the data tools your team already uses to ChatGPT. ## Qwen3.8-27B Pi Adapter: Hands-On Test vs Base Model — Fahd Mirza

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bvKYINTivcE

Fahd Mirza installs and tests Qwen3.8-27B-pi-adapted, a LoRA adapter trained on top of the base Qwen model, and compares it head to head with the base model. It is the opposite of launch hype: a small community-built add-on and an honest test of what actually changes. It matters for anyone running open models locally, because adapters like this are how open models keep improving after release, and a careful test saves you from guessing whether the trade-offs are worth it.
