## Google EmbeddingGemma 2: Multimodal Open Model for on-Device Embeddings — Fahd Mirza

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IB2CWBASMog

Google's EmbeddingGemma 2 is a small, open, multimodal embedding model that runs entirely on device. Fahd installs and tests it locally, showing what it actually understands rather than reciting benchmark scores. It matters because embeddings power every semantic search and retrieval app, and moving them on-device means private, offline, zero-per-query-cost RAG apps — no API keys, no data leaving your machine.
